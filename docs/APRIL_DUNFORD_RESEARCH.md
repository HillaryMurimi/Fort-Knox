# April Dunford — B2B SaaS positioning research

Research status: preliminary. Video titles and links were discovered, but transcript retrieval was unavailable because the connected video research account ran out of credits. These are hypotheses from Dunford's publicly known positioning framework, not claims that particular videos were reviewed in full.

1. Competitive alternatives: understand what landlords would use if Property Command Center did not exist, including spreadsheets, manual M-Pesa reconciliation and competing property software. Research source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rr7VZYwglUs
2. Differentiated capabilities: identify actual product features that are distinctive relative to those alternatives, and verify them against deployed functionality. Research source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cCw2PazE3vQ
3. Customer value: translate verified capabilities into outcomes such as visibility, reduced manual work or clearer reporting without unsupported savings promises. Research source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OvjrY4_FbEM
4. Best-fit customers: distinguish portfolio operators with complex workflows from landlords who may not need a premium solution. Research source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OMcDFjC_YPA
5. Sales narrative: lead with a recognizable market problem and a defensible differentiation rather than a long feature list. Research source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b-EfETdb_zs
