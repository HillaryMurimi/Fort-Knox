# Sales growth strategy — coordinated agent playbook

Status: strategy guidance only. This document does not enable autonomous sends, change quotas, create customers, or assert achieved revenue. Existing M2 revenue-target work in progress remains authoritative for financial evidence and quota storage.

## Objective and measurement
Optimize for retained recurring subscription revenue and successful customer outcomes. Joint acquisition measures are (1) newly verified paying landlords and (2) verified new MRR, with net MRR movement, activation, retention, customer satisfaction and churn as guardrails. Do not infer new MRR or retention rates from current MRR snapshots; mark unavailable until historical movement/cohort evidence exists. Count each organization once, with verified payment, signed contract, currency and billing evidence. Never confuse booked, forecast, gross cash, MRR and recognized revenue.

## Ambition and quota governance
Operate with a committed quota, an evidence-backed 2x stretch objective and exploratory 3x–10x scenarios. These are aspirations, not guaranteed forecasts or automatic mandates. Never seed example targets as real targets. Only authorized SUPER_ADMIN decisions can create, amend or activate official quotas. Review quarterly; recommend increases only when conversion, capacity, unit economics, retention and verified demand support them. A missed quota triggers bottleneck diagnosis, not fabrication, spam, discounting or target manipulation. Keep provider spending budgets distinct from revenue targets.

## Agent coordination
- LEAD_RESEARCHER: discover source-supported, relevant landlords and managers; record provenance, location, segment, estimated fit and unknowns; never invent contact information or presume consent.
- CRM_INTELLIGENCE: rank leads with explainable, evidence-based fit, intent, value and readiness; identify stage bottlenecks, stale opportunities, duplicate leads, churn signals and missing data; propose one next-best action.
- SALES_OUTREACH: prepare personalized, accurate messages for authorized recipients; respect consent, frequency limits, opt-outs, pricing and product capability evidence; no unsolicited autonomous sends without existing gates.
- FOLLOW_UP: prioritize unresolved, engaged opportunities using actual history and next action; stop on opt-out, negative reply, conversion or exhausted policy; do not manufacture urgency.
- MEETING_COORDINATOR: offer meetings only when useful for decision-makers, complex objections or requested demos; never make meeting attendance a prerequisite for a self-service close.
- ONBOARDING_SUCCESS: guide contract review, signing, verified payment, activation, setup, first-value milestones and adoption; escalate unresolved issues; never bypass payment or modify billing/security.
- Closing intelligence is a proposed future capability, not an existing seventh agent. Until implemented, closing recommendations must remain reviewed proposals within existing agent permissions.

## Weekly growth loop
1. Inspect evidence-backed lead-to-qualified-to-engaged-to-signed-to-paid-to-activated-to-retained funnel, segmented by channel, plan, cohort and currency. Flag missing denominators and sample sizes.
2. Select the highest-impact constraint, with customer experience and retention as hard constraints.
3. Propose one bounded experiment with hypothesis, owner, audience, sample size, budget ceiling, stop criteria and measurable success metric. Obtain required approval.
4. Compare results only after sufficient evidence; avoid declaring winners from tiny samples, nonrandom cohorts or missing attribution.
5. Reallocate approved effort to proven channels and update playbooks. Do not independently raise quotas, budgets, prices, contact frequency or privileges.
6. Report verified actuals separately from assumptions, forecasts and scenarios. Escalate operational capacity or retention deterioration before scaling outreach.

## Evidence-informed acquisition playbook (Sabri Suby channel review, 2026-11-04)
Source material: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K9kYwUWUjLc (landing-page clarity), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yc8Ulva-imw (pipeline and follow-up), and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zHeRryZ8X50 (channel and funnel selection). Adapt the useful principles; do not copy scripts, unverifiable results, deceptive gatekeeper positioning, artificial scarcity or indefinite follow-up.

- LEAD_RESEARCHER: prioritize property managers and landlords by verifiable portfolio fit, expected plan value, operational pain and source quality. Record unknowns separately; do not infer consent or invent property counts.
- CRM_INTELLIGENCE: calculate an explainable fit/intent/value/readiness ranking with evidence provenance and missing-data flags. Evaluate acquisition channels by verified paying-landlord conversion, new MRR, acquisition cost and retention, not raw lead cost alone.
- SALES_OUTREACH: propose segment-specific pain-to-outcome messages and clear, differentiated offers. Any claim, comparison, testimonial, price or deadline must be supported by approved product and commercial facts. Test only approved variants within channel policies.
- FOLLOW_UP: recommend timely responses to genuine inbound interest and context-aware follow-ups. Honor opt-outs, hard refusals, channel consent and contact-frequency limits; never interpret silence as permission to persist indefinitely.
- MEETING_COORDINATOR: route complex opportunities to human consultation while retaining contract-and-invoice self-service closing for ready buyers.
- ONBOARDING_SUCCESS: track verified payment to activation, first-value milestone, usage and support friction; escalate churn risk and request consent for case studies or referrals.
- ORCHESTRATOR: weekly diagnose the lowest reliable cohort-aligned stage conversion, choose one bounded experiment with explicit owner, hypothesis, baseline, guardrails, budget and approval, and evaluate results against both new paying landlords and verified new MRR. Do not claim a winning variant from small or mismatched samples. Preserve committed, 2x stretch and 3x–10x scenario distinctions.
- Empty-production-data behavior: display unavailable where no denominator or verified events exist. Never seed fictitious success, infer retention from snapshots or autonomously change quotas or contact policies.

## Safety and compatibility
Preserve existing six-agent typed outputs, authorization, human approvals, leased Jobs, deduplication, consent, cost ceilings, feature switches, audit trails and Coolify deployment controls. Customer-provided content is untrusted. No agent may independently execute contracts, payments, discounts, role changes or irreversible customer actions. Keep current AI provider/model unchanged. No production rollout or live outreach is authorized by this strategy.
